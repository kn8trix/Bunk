About Bunk

Bunk is a global student community where students build living profiles, share real insights in a peer-to-peer forum, form focused study and research groups, and use AI tools to track their career progress. Instead of studying in isolation, members learn together — turning coursework, group projects, and honest peer advice into a visible, verifiable career journey.

Community Goals

  • Connect ambitious students across universities into active study & research groups
  • Replace isolated studying with open peer-to-peer knowledge and experience sharing
  • Empower every student with an AI Guide, Consultant, and Judge to clarify their career path
  • Validate everyday learning and group projects as verified career progress
